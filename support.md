# Kaboom Blocks — Support

## How to play

Drag a block onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it blasts. The blocks
**above** a blast then fall, and if they land in a way that fills another line, that line
blasts too — a chain. The deeper the chain, the more it scores.

Three pieces are dealt at a time and they cannot be rotated. At least one of them always
fits somewhere. When none of them fits any longer, the run ends.

## Questions people actually ask

**Why did my blocks not fall?**
Only blocks that were sitting above a blasted cell fall. Everything else stays exactly where
you put it, even with nothing underneath. That rule is deliberate: it lets you build a ledge
and bring it down later, which is how the big chains happen.

**What is the badge that appears while I drag?**
It is telling you the drop is worth a chain, and how deep: ×2, ×3, ×4. The lines that will
blast light up, and the blocks the fall will take are tinted.

**How do I get a bomb?**
Reach a ×3 chain. You can hold two. A bomb blows a 3×3 hole and the blocks above it fall,
so it can start a chain of its own. Bombs are earned by playing and are never for sale.

**I ran out of moves. What is the revive?**
Once per run, you can clear the bottom two rows and carry on with your score intact. Once
per run only — a high score should mean one unbroken run.

**What is the Daily Blast?**
A hand-built board with a fixed set of three pieces and one target: the deepest chain that
board can produce. Everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day. Solving it extends your
streak. Reach it from the pause menu or the end-of-run screen.

**I bought Remove Ads and got a new phone.**
Pause menu → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

**Is my score saved anywhere I can lose it?**
It is saved on the device and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Telling us the device, the iOS version and what you were doing when it happened makes it
much easier to fix.

# Privacy Policy for Kaboom Blocks

**Last updated:** 14 November 2026

## The short version

Kaboom Blocks does not collect, transmit or share personal data. The game keeps some
statistics about how you play, and they stay on your device. There are no third-party
trackers and no analytics services.

## What the app stores on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the game saves:

- Your best score and your biggest chain
- How many games you have finished
- Your sound and vibration settings
- Whether you have bought Remove Ads
- Your Daily Blast results: the best chain you reached on each day's puzzle, and your streak
- A record of your recent runs — score, score at the moment you ran out of moves, biggest
  chain, blocks blasted, pieces placed, how long the run lasted, how full the board was, and
  whether you took the revive. The most recent 300 runs are kept; older ones are discarded.

That last one exists so the game's difficulty can be tuned against how people actually play.
It contains no identifiers of any kind: no advertising ID, no device ID, no account, no
name, and no clock time more precise than the day. **It never leaves your device.** If we
ever send any of it anywhere, this policy will say so before that version ships, and it will
still carry nothing that identifies you.

## Purchases

Kaboom Blocks offers one in-app purchase, Remove Ads. Purchases are handled entirely by
Apple. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether you own the purchase
and stores only the yes-or-no answer on the device.

## Advertising

**This version of the app contains no advertising and no advertising SDK.**

If a future version introduces ads, it will come with an updated policy naming the ad
provider and what it collects, an updated privacy label on the App Store, and the tracking
permission prompt Apple requires. Buying Remove Ads will always remove them.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone,
children included. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no social features.

## Network access

Apart from Apple's own App Store services for the in-app purchase, the app makes no network
requests. With no purchase to restore, it works entirely offline.

## Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of it. There is
nothing held anywhere else, and no account to close.

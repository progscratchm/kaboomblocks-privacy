# Privacy Policy for Kaboom Blocks

**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

## The short version

The game itself keeps statistics about how you play, and those stay on your device. The one
thing that does leave the device is advertising: Kaboom Blocks shows ads through Google
AdMob, which collects data to choose them. Buying Remove Ads stops that entirely — the ad
software is not even started for a player who has bought it.

## What the app stores on your device

To remember your progress between sessions, the game saves:

- Your best score and your biggest chain
- How many games you have finished
- Your sound and vibration settings
- Whether you have bought Remove Ads
- Your Daily Blast results: the best chain you reached on each day's puzzle, and your streak
- A record of your recent runs — score, score at the moment you ran out of moves, biggest
  chain, blocks blasted, pieces placed, how long the run lasted, how full the board was, and
  whether you took the revive. The most recent 300 runs are kept; older ones are discarded.

That last one exists so the game's difficulty can be tuned against how people actually play.
It contains no identifiers of any kind: no advertising ID, no device ID, no account, no
name, and no clock time more precise than the day. **It never leaves your device.** If we
ever send any of it anywhere, this policy will say so before that version ships, and it will
still carry nothing that identifies you.

## Purchases

Kaboom Blocks offers one in-app purchase, Remove Ads. Purchases are handled entirely by
Apple. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether you own the purchase
and stores only the yes-or-no answer on the device.

## Advertising

Kaboom Blocks shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Two places only: a full-screen ad between
runs, no more than one every three runs and never within two minutes of the last, and a
rewarded ad you can choose to watch to revive a run. There are no banner ads, and nothing
ever interrupts a run in progress.

To choose those ads, Google collects data from your device. What it collects is described in
Google's own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites — and, where the
law requires it, you will be asked first. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you get Google's
consent form before any ad loads. On iOS you are also asked, separately, whether the app may
use your device's advertising identifier. Saying no to either is fine: the game is identical,
and you simply get less relevant ads.

**Buying Remove Ads stops all of it.** The advertising software is not started at all for a
player who owns that purchase, so there is nothing for it to collect.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages, and we collect no personal information from anyone
ourselves. There is no chat and no user-generated content. The only social feature is Apple's
Game Center, described below, and it is optional.

The app is not in Apple's Kids Category, because it shows third-party advertising, and we
would rather say that plainly than claim a label we do not qualify for.

## Game Center leaderboards

The game has three optional leaderboards — best score, biggest chain and Daily Blast streak —
run by Apple's Game Center. They work only if you are signed in to Game Center on your device,
and the game never asks you to sign in: the sign-in screen appears only if you tap
Leaderboards yourself.

If you are signed in, the game sends Apple three numbers — your best score, your biggest chain
and your day streak — so they can be ranked against other players. Your Game Center nickname
is the name shown on the board, and it is the one you chose with Apple, not anything the game
knows about you. We do not receive your Apple Account, your email address or your real name,
and the game never reads other players' details.

Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. You can leave
the leaderboards at any time in Settings → Game Center, and simply never opening them means
nothing is ever sent.

## Network access

The app talks to three things: Apple's App Store services, for the purchase; Apple's Game
Center, for the leaderboards, if you are signed in to it; and Google's ad servers, for the
ads. Nothing else. Your statistics and your individual Daily Blast results are never sent
anywhere, and with Game Center signed out no score leaves the device at all.

## Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of it. There is
nothing held anywhere else, and no account to close.
